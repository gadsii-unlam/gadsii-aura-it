# Product photo credits

Internal record of where every product photo comes from. It is not shown in the
app and is excluded from the build (`angular.json` → assets `ignore`).

All files are 640×480 JPEG crops of the originals:

- **Pexels files:** cropped and compressed by the Pexels CDN (`w=640&h=480&fit=crop`).
- **Wikimedia Commons files:** center-cropped to 4:3 and resized locally.
